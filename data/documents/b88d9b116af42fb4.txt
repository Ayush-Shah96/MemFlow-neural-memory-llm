Civic Services Reform Act

The Civic Services Reform Act was drafted after negotiations between the Federal Reform Coalition and the National Development Party.
Elena Marlow advocated stronger transparency requirements.
Marcus Hale proposed additional regional infrastructure funding.
Sofia Bennett coordinated the final parliamentary amendments.

The act was passed in 2022.
Its implementation framework created regional digital service grants.
Local councils could apply for grants to modernize public-service systems.

Daniel Reed later joined the implementation review committee.
The committee published its first progress report in 2023.
