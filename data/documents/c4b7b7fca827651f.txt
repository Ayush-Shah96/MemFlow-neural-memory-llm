National Development Party

The National Development Party was led by Marcus Hale from 2019 to 2023.
Marcus Hale appointed Sofia Bennett as parliamentary affairs coordinator.
Sofia Bennett coordinated discussions with the Federal Reform Coalition.

The National Development Party supported the Civic Services Reform Act after several amendments.
The party proposed additional funding for regional infrastructure.
Marcus Hale presented the amended proposal during a parliamentary session in 2022.

After the 2022 election, Sofia Bennett became minister for regional administration.
She continued work on the Civic Services Reform Act and its implementation framework.
