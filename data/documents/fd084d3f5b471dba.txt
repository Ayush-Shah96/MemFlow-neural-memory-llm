Federal Reform Coalition

The Federal Reform Coalition was formed in 2018 by Elena Marlow and Daniel Reed.
Elena Marlow became the coalition's first chairperson.
Daniel Reed served as policy director.

The coalition proposed the Civic Services Reform Act in 2020.
The proposal focused on digital public services, government transparency, and local administrative funding.
In 2021, the coalition joined negotiations with the National Development Party.

Elena Marlow supported a parliamentary committee to review the reform proposal.
Daniel Reed prepared the coalition's policy brief for the committee.
The coalition later worked with regional councils to revise its funding provisions.
