Parliamentary Review of Civic Services Reform

The parliamentary review of the Civic Services Reform Act began in 2024.
Sofia Bennett presented evidence from regional councils.
Daniel Reed presented findings from the implementation review committee.

The review concluded that regional digital service grants had improved access to several public services.
It recommended continuing the grant program with stronger auditing requirements.

Elena Marlow welcomed the review and called for greater transparency in grant reporting.
Marcus Hale supported the auditing recommendation.
Priya Sen submitted evidence about the Riverland Regional Council's experience.

The recommendations were sent to the parliamentary committee for consideration.
